## AI answers (`api/ask.mjs`, 2026-10-04)
- How it works: Jev (`~typesafe/jev-latest`, OpenRouter decisions API, about $0.00003 a call) picks a KB topic and a difficulty. Below 0.75 confidence, it loads every topic.
- Models: lookup questions use `google/gemini-2.5-flash` ($0.30/$2.50 per million tokens, about 0.1 cent a question). Reasoning questions use `anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5` ($2/$10, about 1 to 2 cents), with `reasoning.effort: low`.
- Knowledge base: `kb/*.md` and `api/_kb.mjs` are generated by `python tools/build_kb.py` from the route pages. Re-run it after editing any route.
- Evals: `node evals/run_ask.mjs [baseUrl]` with `KEY_ENV_FILE=<env file>`. The expanded suite has 39 questions, including Tagalog and Korean. Use `MATCH=<regex>` for focused checks. The old 37/37 baseline used weaker grading; the current checks require valid external citations and complete streams. `python evals/route_test.py` checks Jev routing alone. See `QUALITY.md` for regression commands and release gates.
- Key: `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` in Vercel env, which Adam adds himself. Without it the endpoint returns 503 `not_configured`.

## Codex quality pass (2026-10-04, local changes)
- Keyboard-contained, modal menu; skip links and main landmarks; visible focus rings; reduced-motion support; corrected hidden cards and touch targets.
- Canonical and sharing metadata now use PermitGU throughout, with `social-preview.png`. The homepage loads photos on demand and has a branded fallback.
- AI answers have upstream and browser timeouts, cancellation, invalid-citation and incomplete-stream errors, numbered external sources, and safer retry states. The local dev server binds only to loopback and refuses env files and API source files.
- Shared rate limiting is implemented in `api/_rate.mjs`, but needs Adam to configure `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL` and `UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN`. Until then, limits remain best effort per instance.
- `corrections.html` prepares copyable local drafts only. No inbox, form submission, personal contact details, or government partnership claims were added.
- Source research dates are visible. The typhoon route now links to the official 2026 Compiler of Laws PDF and distinguishes the owner-only roof-covering exemption from the broader nonstructural repair exemption.
- Agency facts, fluent-speaker review, and real-user testing remain human work. Do not describe machine translations or automated citation checks as verified legal advice.

## Design standard: america.gov (Adam, 2026-10-03)
america.gov is the standard. Mirror its UI/UX and design: a huge, tight serif headline, lots of white space, deep navy ink `#000c1f`, one big "describe what you need" box with example prompts, pill controls, and rounded cards. Swap the identity for Guam (favicon, flag, Guam greeting), and never use America branding. Check design changes against the live america.gov.
