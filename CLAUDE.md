# CLAUDE.md - guamgov

Unofficial demo of a Guam permit guide modeled on PermitSF (sf.gov/permitsf), built 2026-10-02 at Adam's dad's request. Static HTML pages plus `styles.css`, no build step, no template (trivial-page exception).
Live: https://guamgov.vercel.app. Keep the "unofficial demo" banner on every page. Every step links to its official source, and anything unconfirmed goes in a yellow "Not yet confirmed" box. See `GOAL.md` for scope and the questions for Dad.

## Design standard: america.gov (Adam, 2026-10-03)
america.gov is the standard. Mirror its UI/UX and design: a huge, tight serif headline, lots of white space, deep navy ink `#000c1f`, one big "describe what you need" box with example prompts, pill controls, and rounded cards. Swap the identity for Guam (favicon, flag, Guam greeting), and never use America branding. Check design changes against the live america.gov.
