About

PermitGU is an unofficial demo of what a single front door to the Government of Guam could look like. It was built in October 2026 at the request of a Guam resident who saw America.gov launch and asked: why not Guam?

Inspired by

  • America.gov, which brings the federal government together in one place, starting from what you need.
  • PermitSF (sf.gov/permitsf), San Francisco’s plain-language guide that turns permits into step-by-step routes.

What it is not

PermitGU is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of Guam or any of its agencies. It does not process applications or payments. Always confirm requirements with the agency before you apply.

Help make it right

The routes are only as good as what people on island know. If you work at an agency, or you have been through one of these processes, the “Still unknown” lists on each route are exactly where your knowledge helps.
