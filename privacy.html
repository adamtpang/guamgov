Privacy

PermitGU has no accounts, no logins, and no forms that collect your personal details. Here is exactly what happens when you visit.

What we count

We use PostHog to count anonymous page views and when you leave a page, so we know which routes are useful. Session recording, surveys, and automatic click tracking are turned off. We do not build a profile of you.

What you type in the search box

Search happens in your browser. What you type is not sent to us.

Other services the page loads

  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run the service.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, and homepage photos load from Unsplash. Both receive a normal web request from your browser.
  • Links to agency sites take you to those sites, which have their own privacy practices.

Is anything shared with the government?

No. This is an unofficial demo. Nothing you do here is shared with the Government of Guam.
