# Other Government of Guam services

Topic id: `services`. Official sites for common tasks that are not permit routes.

- Renew a driver's license or ID: Guam online renewal and appointments. Official site: https://myid.guam.gov/ (keywords: driver license id renew real dmv car)
- Pay a government bill: PayGuam online payments. Official site: https://pay.guam.gov/ (keywords: pay bill fee payment government)
- Typhoon and emergency updates: Homeland Security / Civil Defense. Official site: https://ghs.guam.gov/ (keywords: typhoon emergency disaster storm shelter alert civil defense)
- Find work or labor help: Department of Labor. Official site: https://dol.guam.gov/ (keywords: job work labor employment hire)
- Public schools: Guam Department of Education. Official site: https://gdoe.net/ (keywords: school education student enroll)
- Power service and outages: Guam Power Authority. Official site: https://www.guampowerauthority.com/ (keywords: power electric bill outage)
- Check a contractor's license: Contractors License Board. Official site: https://clb.guam.gov/contractors-listing/ (keywords: contractor license verify check)
- Taxes: Department of Revenue and Taxation. Official site: https://www.guamtax.com/ (keywords: tax taxes return revenue)

## Other homepage topics

- Open or renew a business license: File Form BL-01 with Rev & Tax, which routes it to every agency that must clear it. Renewals can be done online. Sources: [How to apply](https://www.govguamdocs.com/revtax/docs/PROCEDURESANDREQUIREMENTSFORNEWBL.pdf); [Renew online](https://www.guamtax.com/efile/biz.html) (keywords: business license shop store company sell open small renew)
- Land, zoning, and surveys: DLM's Division of Planning supports the Guam Land Use Commission and reviews permit clearances. Sources: [DLM Planning](https://dlm.guam.gov/division-of-planning/) (keywords: land survey zoning subdivision lot property variance check zoning)
