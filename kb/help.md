# Get help, check a permit, or appeal

Topic id: `help`. Page: https://permitgu.vercel.app/help

Where to go in person, how to ask about a permit you already filed, what to do if you are turned down, and who to call.

## The short version

- Most permit agencies sit together at the Business License and Permit Center in Upper Tumon
- We found no online status tracker for Guam building permits. Call or email the permit center
- A building permit decision can be appealed to the Guam Land Use Commission within 15 calendar days

## Step 1: Get help in person

Who: Business License and Permit Center (BLPC), the “one stop”

- Where: on the Department of Public Works compound, 542 North Marine Corps Drive, Upper Tumon (Tamuning), Guam 96913
- What it does: one place to get or renew a business license and to apply for a building permit
- Who is there: Public Works (DPW), Public Health (DPHSS), Land Management (DLM), Revenue and Taxation (business license, collections, real property), Guam EPA, Guam Fire Department, and the Treasurer of Guam
- Counter phones listed by the center: DPW customer service (671) 646-3104, Rev and Tax (671) 646-3102, Fire (671) 646-8810, Land Management (671) 647-0854, Public Health (671) 646-1276, Guam EPA (671) 647-GEPA, Treasurer (671) 646-3150
- Email: BLPC@dpw.guam.gov. DPW’s main line is (671) 646-3131/3232
- Land Management is not at the counter right now: the center says DLM is temporarily handling all applications and clearances at its headquarters, ITC Building, Suite 733, 590 South Marine Corps Drive, Tamuning. Phone (671) 649-5263

## Step 2: Check a permit’s status

Who: By agency

- Building permit (DPW): we found no online lookup. The GovBLPC page says the government is procuring a system to put building, occupancy, and clearing and grading permits online, so it is not live yet. Ask the DPW counter at (671) 646-3104 or BLPC@dpw.guam.gov, and have your permit number ready
- Business license (Rev and Tax): you can renew an existing business license and print it online at GuamTax.com. You need your EIN or SSN, your gross receipts tax account number, and an email address. New licenses are filed at the permit center
- Guam EPA: Guam EPA runs a permitting site at permits.epa.guam.gov. For questions, its One Stop permit window is at 17-3304 Mariner Avenue, Tiyan, Barrigada, phone (671) 588-4751
- Zoning, land use, and land records (DLM): we found no online case tracker. Call DLM at (671) 649-5263
- Health permits (DPHSS): no online lookup found, and the DPHSS website is being rebuilt. Call Environmental Health at (671) 922-2533, the number printed on the sanitary permit application

## Step 3: Appeal a decision or make a complaint

Who: Depends on who decided

- Building permit refused, or another DPW building official decision: appeal to the Guam Land Use Commission. File a written notice of appeal, giving your reasons, with both the building official and the Commission within 15 calendar days (Sundays and holidays count). The Commission can pause the matter, holds a hearing, and decides in writing (21 GCA §§66606 to 66613, and §61615)
- Disputes over alternate materials or how the building code is read: these go to the Guam Building Code Council sitting as the Board of Appeals. Its final decision can be taken to the Superior Court (21 GCA §66508(a))
- Guam Land Use Commission decisions on a variance: ask the Superior Court to review it within 15 days after the decision is filed with DLM and DPW (21 GCA §61621)
- Guam EPA septic or wastewater permit denied: ask the Guam EPA Board of Directors for a hearing in writing within 15 days of the official denial notice (22 GAR §12105(k))
- Business license denied: send a written request to the License Board to review the denial. A suspension or revocation can be reviewed the same way. Board decisions can be appealed to the Superior Court (11 GCA §§70113 and 70114)
