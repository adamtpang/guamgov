# Work on a road, sidewalk, or driveway

Topic id: `public-spaces`. Page: https://permitgu.vercel.app/public-spaces

Digging in, building on, or placing anything in a public road needs a written permit from Public Works. Water, sewer, and power hookups go through GWA and GPA. Here is what the law and the utilities ask for.

## You will end up with

- A DPW permit for work in the road right of way
- A road restored to DPW standards, and your deposit back
- GWA water or sewer and GPA power connected to your lot

## Step 1: Check whether your work needs a DPW highway permit

Who: Department of Public Works, Highways Division

- A "roadway" in the law is the entire width of the right of way, not just the paved lanes the public drives on (5 GCA §53101)
- You need a written DPW permit to: make an opening or excavation in a highway; place, change, or renew an encroachment (a pipe, pole, fence, sign, building, or other structure in, under, or over the highway); put an advertising sign in a highway; or plant, cut, or remove a tree or plant in a highway (5 GCA §53102)
- Doing any of these without a valid permit is a petty misdemeanor, and DPW may remove an unpermitted sign right away (§53102)
- DPW Highway Encroachment and Permit section: (671) 649-3159 or 649-3104. Rights-of-way: 649-3144 or 649-3146

## Step 2: Apply with a work schedule and a deposit

Who: You or your contractor, at DPW Highways

- Every application to excavate a road needs a schedule of work with a deadline for restoring the road to its original condition or better (§53105(a))
- Deposit: on top of the application fee, an excavation needs a deposit of $500 or 5% of the cost of the work affecting the road, whichever is more. A performance bond from a surety licensed on Guam can stand in its place (§53105(b))
- DPW may also require a bond and may supervise the work, at your cost (§§53104, 53105(e))
- Permits can be revoked for cause on 30 days' written notice (§53103)

## Step 3: Do the work, tell the mayor, and restore the road

Who: You or your contractor

- Tell your village mayor before digging starts, and again when the road is restored (§53105(f))
- Close the opening and restore the road by the date on your permit (§53105(a))
- DPW inspects the repair and keeps the deposit for up to 3 months after it is done. If the repair is not fixed within 14 days of DPW's notice, DPW can hire the work out and take the cost from your deposit or bond (§53105(b)(4))
- You warranty the repair for 1 year, or 90 days on an unpaved road. A good repair gets the deposit back in full; the application fee is not refunded (§53105(b)(4)(D))
- Emergencies: you may dig first only to prevent imminent danger or fix an unexpected service outage, then get the permit on the next working day. If DPW decides it was not an emergency, the penalty is $500 (§53105(d))

Sources:
- [5 GCA §53105](https://col.guamcourts.gov/sites/default/files/5gc053.pdf)

## Step 4: Connect water, sewer, and power

Who: Guam Waterworks Authority (GWA) and Guam Power Authority (GPA)

- GWA water: apply at GWA Customer Service any time during construction, ideally before your contractor works in the right of way. Bring the customer information form, your certificate of title or deed of gift, your building permit, a map to the site, and an authorization if you are not the owner
- GWA gives you an account and work order number, then you fill out a Permit to Tap with the Permits and Inspections staff. Give GWA 48 hours' notice before trenching, meter installation, or tapping
- After inspection you get a Job Cost Sheet. Once paid, GWA says the meter is typically installed in 3 to 5 working days
- GWA sewer: the same steps, with a Wastewater Connection Permit and 48 hours' notice before trenching and connecting
- GWA Permits, Inspection and Enforcement: (671) 300-6058. Customer Service: (671) 647-7800
- GPA power: apply at the GPA payment center in Fadian, the Hagåtña satellite office, or GWA in upper Tumon, or by email. Bring the application, a photo ID, your deed, title, or lease with authorization, and a security deposit. The application fee is $25, and residential deposits run $125 to $500. GPA: (671) 647-5787

## Also: Closing a road, or putting something on it?

- Placing a structure, pipe, pole, fence, or sign in, under, or over a road is an encroachment and needs a DPW permit (5 GCA §§53101, 53102)
- DPW can order an unpermitted encroachment removed (§§53106 to 53108)
