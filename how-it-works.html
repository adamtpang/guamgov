How it works

Getting something done with the Government of Guam usually means several offices, several forms, and a lot of asking around. PermitGU turns that into one route per task, in the order you need it.

1. Start with what you want to do

Type what you need, like “start a food truck” or “fix my roof after a typhoon”, and PermitGU shows the matching route or service. Search runs entirely in your browser.

2. Every step comes from an official source

Routes are built by reading the agencies’ own pages, forms, and the Guam Code Annotated, such as the Building Law (21 GCA Chapter 66) and Rev & Tax’s business license procedure. Each step links to where it came from, so you can check it yourself.

3. Gaps are marked, never guessed

If a fee, form, or requirement could not be confirmed from an official source, it appears in a yellow “Not yet confirmed” box instead of being guessed. Each route also lists what is still unknown.

4. Links are checked

Agency links are tested when routes are updated, and the date of the last check is shown on each page.

What PermitGU cannot do yet

It cannot submit forms, take payments, or show the status of your application. Those need the agencies’ own systems. For now, PermitGU tells you where to go and what to bring.
