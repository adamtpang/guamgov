What permit do you need on Guam?

One plain-language starting point for building, business, and environmental permits, so you know which office to go to and what to bring.

Building a house, addition, or fence

Most new construction and major renovation needs a building permit.

  1. Get your lot and zoning confirmed
  2. Prepare plans (licensed designer for larger work)
  3. Submit to Public Works for plan review
  4. Schedule inspections as work progresses
Department of Public Works →

Starting a business

A business license is the usual first step for selling goods or services.

  1. Register your business name
  2. Apply for a business license and tax registration
  3. Check if your activity needs extra clearances
Department of Revenue and Taxation →

Food business or food truck

Food sales usually need a health or sanitary permit on top of the business license.

  1. Get your business license
  2. Apply for a sanitary permit and inspection
  3. Food handler training for staff
Public Health and Social Services →

Land, zoning, and surveys

Zoning questions, land records, and subdivision go through land management.

  1. Look up your lot and zoning
  2. Request records or a survey review
  3. Apply for a zone change or variance if needed
Department of Land Management →

Clearing, grading, or septic

Earth moving, clearing land, and wastewater systems need environmental review.

  1. Describe the site and the work
  2. Apply for the matching environmental permit
  3. Follow erosion and runoff controls
Guam Environmental Protection Agency →
