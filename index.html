Start a food truck or food business

Business license, agency clearances, sanitary permit, health certificates, and fire, in order.

  1. Business license (Form BL-01)
  2. Clearances routed by Rev & Tax
  3. Sanitary permit and inspection
  4. Health certificates for staff
See the full route →
🏠

Build a house, addition, or fence

Building permits are filed at the Business License and Permit Center at Public Works, where DPW, DLM, DPHSS, Guam EPA and Rev & Tax all sign off.

  1. Confirm your lot and zoning
  2. Prepare plans
  3. File at the permit center
  4. Schedule DPW inspections
Permit center →
🏪

Open a small business

Licenses are issued at the same permit center. Rev & Tax sends your application to each agency that must clear it.

  1. File Form BL-01
  2. Get any clearances your activity needs
  3. Pay and pick up your license
How to apply →Rev & Tax →
🗺️

Land, zoning, and surveys

DLM's Division of Planning supports the Guam Land Use Commission and reviews permit clearances.

  1. Look up your lot and zoning
  2. Request records or a survey review
  3. Apply for a zone change or variance
DLM Planning →
🌿

Clear, grade, or install septic

Guam EPA issues the Clearing, Grubbing, Grading and Stockpiling permit and sets septic rules (updated July 2024).

  1. Find the form for your work
  2. Apply on the EPA portal
  3. For septic, read the 2024 rules first
Apply online →Forms →Septic →
