What permit do you need on Guam?

One plain-language starting point for building, business, and environmental permits, so you know which office to go to and what to bring.

Building a house, addition, or fence

Building permits are filed at the Business License and Permit Center (BLPC) at Public Works, a one-stop desk shared by DPW, DLM, DPHSS, Guam EPA and Rev & Tax.

  1. Confirm your lot and zoning with Land Management
  2. Prepare plans (licensed designer for larger work)
  3. File the building permit at the BLPC, where each agency signs off on its clearance
  4. Schedule DPW building inspections as work progresses

Starting a business

Business licenses are issued or renewed at the same BLPC hub, where Rev & Tax handles licensing and tax registration.

  1. Apply for a business license at the BLPC
  2. Get any clearances your activity needs (health, EPA, zoning)
  3. Register for taxes with Rev & Tax
Business License and Permit Center → · Rev & Tax →

Food business or food truck

Restaurants, food trucks, and temporary event food sellers all need a sanitary permit from DPHSS Environmental Health, issued after plan review and inspection.

  1. Get your business license
  2. Apply for a sanitary permit (DEH, 2nd floor, Central Public Health, Mangilao)
  3. Pass the DEH inspection
  4. Staff get a health certificate (food safety course)

Land, zoning, and surveys

DLM's Division of Planning supports the Guam Land Use Commission and reviews permit clearances.

  1. Look up your lot and zoning
  2. Request records or a survey review
  3. Apply for a zone change or variance through Planning if needed
DLM Division of Planning →

Clearing, grading, or septic

Guam EPA issues the Clearing, Grubbing, Grading and Stockpiling permit and sets septic rules (updated July 2024 under P.L. 37-105).

  1. Find the form for your work type
  2. Apply online through the Guam EPA permit portal
  3. For septic, read the 2024 requirements first
